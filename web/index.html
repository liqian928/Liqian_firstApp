Traveling with your dog

Start the paperwork early.

Dogs flying in from China need a rabies antibody test and a 90-day wait before they can enter Switzerland. For a late-June arrival, that means starting in winter.

Entry timeline, counted back from late June

  1. By Jan 2027Confirm the ISO 15-digit microchip, implanted before the rabies shot. Book the vet.
  2. By mid-FebRabies vaccination, recorded against the microchip number.
  3. By mid-MarBlood draw for the antibody (titer) test, at least 30 days after the shot, tested at an EU-approved lab. The result must be 0.5 IU/ml or higher.
  4. 90 daysMandatory wait counted from the blood draw date.
  5. 3 wks beforeApply for the Swiss import permit (FSVO/BLV) for a dog arriving from a rabies-risk country.
  6. Final 10 daysOfficial veterinary health certificate. Fly into Zurich, Geneva or Basel only, and declare at customs.

Dogs on Swiss transport

  • Most trains, boats and buses: a Day Pass for Dogs covers everything for a full day in 1st or 2nd class. It costs CHF 25, rising to CHF 27 from 13 Dec 2026. Dogs under 30 cm at the shoulder ride free in a carrier.
  • Jungfrau region: dogs ride free to Grindelwald, Lauterbrunnen, Mürren and First. Eigergletscher to Jungfraujoch needs a paid dog ticket, sold at stations only.
  • Gornergrat: dogs ride free.
  • Glacier Express: No dogs Food is served throughout the train. Guide dogs are the only exception.
  • Dining cars and themed boat cruises: no dogs.
  • Italian Greyhound note: at 3,000 m there's snow and cold wind even in June. Pack a warm coat and booties.

Not dog-friendly on this plan

  • No dogs Glacier Express. Use regional trains on the same line, or a private driver.
  • No dogs The Omnia, Zermatt, which bans pets and service animals. Stay at Riffelalp Resort instead.
  • No dogs Tandem paragliding and hotel spas.
  • Check ahead Matterhorn helicopter flight, Glacier 3000, Oeschinensee gondola, Pilatus, private boat charters.
  • Check ahead Going home: confirm China's pet re-entry rules (GACC) before you leave.

Before you go

Book these first.

Booking order

  1. NowRiffelalp or The Omnia, Bürgenstock, The Alpina, Badrutt's Palace, The Dolder Grand. Late June fills early.
  2. EarlyGlacier Express Excellence Class: one car, very few seats. It's about CHF 540 on top of a 1st-class ticket (2026 price).
  3. ~3 monthsGlacier Express 1st class seat reservations and the helicopter flight.
  4. 1 monthSwiss Travel Pass, 1st class, 12 days, plus SBB luggage forwarding between hotels.

Late June, practically

  • Light: sunrise is around 5:30. Book rooms facing the peaks and take the first lift up.
  • Snow: upper trails like the top of the Five Lakes Walk can still be snowy. Bring waterproof boots.
  • Weather: afternoon storms build in the mountains. Shoot in the morning and save afternoons for lakes, spas and shopping.
  • Layers: it can be 28°C in Lucerne and near freezing at Jungfraujoch on the same day.
  • Gstaad: high season starts in July, so confirm boutique and hotel opening dates.

Shopping stops

  • Gstaad Promenade: car-free street of designer boutiques and jewelers.
  • St. Moritz, Via Serlas: luxury fashion and watches, right outside Badrutt's.
  • Zurich, Bahnhofstrasse: Swiss watchmakers, flagship stores, chocolatiers.
  • Lucerne old town: watch and jewelry houses along the Schwanenplatz.
